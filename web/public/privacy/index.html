1. Who we are

LoopMeet is made and run by Palehorse, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers the LoopMeet apps for Android and iOS, and this website.

We don't sell your personal data, we don't use it for advertising, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. We use your data only to run LoopMeet for you and the people in your groups.

4. Location

LoopMeet asks for your device's location only when you search for a meetup place, and only if you allow it. If you allow precise location, this can be your exact position, from GPS. On devices that let you choose, you can allow approximate location instead.

We send your location, with your search text, through our service to Google Places, so nearby places come first. Your location isn't stored by LoopMeet, and it's never used to track you. A meetup's place is stored because you chose it, not because of where you were. You can turn location access off in your device settings at any time, and place search still works.

5. Push notifications

If you allow notifications, LoopMeet tells you about new and changed meetups in your groups, and reminds you before they start. OneSignal sends them, through Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android and Apple's push service on iPhone.

You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time. We keep records of which notifications were delivered and opened, so the same one isn't sent twice.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while you have a LoopMeet account. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete Account) or on the Account page of this website.

The 30 days after you ask

When you ask to delete your account, permanent deletion is scheduled for 30 days later, and you see the date before you confirm. From that moment, your profile, the groups you own, your memberships, meetups you created and invitations you sent or received are hidden from everyone else, and no notifications are sent to you or about anything you own.

During those 30 days you can change your mind: sign in, in the app or here, and choose Restore account. Everything comes back exactly as it was.

Permanent deletion

When the 30 days are up, we delete your account within 24 hours of the date, usually within an hour. That removes:

  • your push-notification registration with OneSignal
  • your Sign in with Apple authorization, which we revoke with Apple
  • your profile photos, including earlier ones
  • the groups you own, with their meetups, members and invitations; your memberships in other groups; meetups you created; invitations you sent or received; your devices and notification records; your profile
  • your sign-in account itself, with its email, password hash and linked Google or Apple accounts

After that, LoopMeet keeps no copy of your data, not even a record that you had an account. Your email address can be used to sign up again, as a new, empty account. We keep anonymous notification records: a notification's type, the identifiers of a group or meetup that no longer exists, and times. They don't identify anyone.

What providers keep for a short time

  • Supabase database backups hold a full copy of the database and are kept for 7 days.
  • Supabase logs, such as user identifiers and IP addresses, are kept for 7 days.
  • Our service's own logs, hosted by DigitalOcean, aren't stored.
  • OneSignal deletes your registration asynchronously after we ask, on its own schedule.
  • Apple keeps its own record that you used Sign in with Apple with LoopMeet, under Apple's privacy policy.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: your profile is in the app under Profile, where you can change your display name and photo. Email us to correct anything else.
  • Delete: in the app under Profile → Delete Account, or on the Account page.
  • Location and notifications: turn either off at any time in your device settings.
  • Anything else: depending on where you live, you may have the right to a copy of your data, to object to how it's used, or to complain to a data protection authority. Email info@palehorse.net and we'll reply within 30 days.

8. Children

LoopMeet isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and its "Last updated" date before the change takes effect.
