Support

Answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, email info@palehorse.net.

Signing in

Which sign-in methods can I use?

You can sign in with an email and password, with Google, or with Apple on iPhone. Use the method you signed up with, so you reach the same account.

I forgot my password.

Email info@palehorse.net from the address on your account and we'll help you get back in. If you signed in with Google or Apple, you don't have a LoopMeet password: sign in with that account instead.

Groups and invitations

How do I invite someone to a group?

Open the group and invite them by email. The invitation appears in their Invitations tab when they sign in to LoopMeet with that email address.

I was invited but I don't see the invitation.

Invitations are sent to an email address. Check that you're signed in with the address you were invited at, or ask the person who invited you to check it.

Notifications

How do I turn notifications on or off?

In the app, go to Profile and open the notification settings, which take you to your device's settings for LoopMeet. Location and notifications can each be turned off at any time.

Deleting your account

How do I delete my account?

You can do it either way:

  • In the app: go to Profile → Delete Account, review what will be deleted, then type DELETE to confirm.
  • On the web: sign in on the Account page with the method you use in the app, and follow the same steps.
What happens next?

Your account is scheduled for permanent deletion 30 days later, and you see the date before you confirm. Straight away, your profile and the groups you own are hidden from everyone else.

If you change your mind within those 30 days, sign in, in the app or on the Account page, and choose Restore account. Everything comes back as it was.

After 30 days your data is permanently deleted. The privacy policy lists exactly what's removed and what providers keep for a short time.

I can't sign in any more. How do I delete my account?

Email info@palehorse.net from the address on your account and ask us to delete it.
