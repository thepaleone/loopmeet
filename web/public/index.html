Meetups for the people you actually see

LoopMeet keeps your groups in the loop. Make a group, invite your friends, and plan when and where you'll meet, all in one place.

  • Groups

    Climbing crew, book club, the Friday-night regulars. Start a group for each, and see who's in it.

  • Invitations

    Invite people by email. The invitation waits in the app for them when they sign in with that address.

  • Meetups

    Pick a time and a place, and everyone in the group sees it on their home screen.

  • Reminders

    Optional notifications let your group know about new meetups, changes and what's coming up.
